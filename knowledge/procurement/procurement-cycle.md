# Engineering Procurement Cycle

## Material Requisition (MR)

The **MR** is the engineering package that defines what to buy: scope of
supply, datasheets, specifications, drawings, quantities, inspection and
testing requirements, spare parts, and the vendor documents required.
Engineering issues the MR for inquiry, and later for purchase.

## Request for Quotation (RFQ)

Procurement sends the MR, plus commercial terms, to approved vendors and
asks them to bid.

## Bid evaluation

- **TBE — Technical Bid Evaluation**: engineering checks each offer against
  the MR and specifications, lists deviations and clarifications, and
  declares offers technically acceptable or not.
- **CBE — Commercial Bid Evaluation**: procurement compares price,
  delivery, payment terms and commercial conditions of technically
  acceptable bidders.

## Purchase Order (PO)

The PO is the contract with the selected vendor, incorporating the final
MR revision and all agreed clarifications.

## After award

- **VDRL / VDR (Vendor Document Requirements List)** — the list of
  documents the vendor must submit (drawings, datasheets, calculations,
  ITP, procedures, manuals) and their review status.
- **Kick-off meeting** — aligns scope, schedule and document submissions.
- **Vendor document review** — engineering comments on vendor drawings,
  typically returned with a status code (e.g. approved, approved with
  comments, revise and resubmit — codes vary by project).
- **Expediting** — follow-up on progress and delivery.
- **Inspection & testing** — per the approved vendor ITP, including
  witness/hold points such as a **FAT (Factory Acceptance Test)**.
- **Release note / shipping** — issued after final inspection.

## Common abbreviations

MR, RFQ, TBE, CBE, PO, VDRL, FAT, SAT (Site Acceptance Test), IRN
(Inspection Release Note), MDR (Manufacturer's Data Report / record book).
