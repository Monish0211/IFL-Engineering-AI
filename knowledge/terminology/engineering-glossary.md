# Engineering Abbreviations Glossary

## General and project

- **EPC** — Engineering, Procurement and Construction
- **FEED** — Front-End Engineering Design
- **IFC** — Issued for Construction
- **RFI** — Request for Information
- **TQ** — Technical Query
- **MOC** — Management of Change
- **MDR** — Manufacturer's Data Report / record book
- **SOW** — Scope of Work

## Process and safety

- **PFD** — Process Flow Diagram
- **P&ID** — Piping and Instrumentation Diagram
- **H&MB** — Heat and Material Balance
- **HAZOP** — Hazard and Operability Study
- **HAZID** — Hazard Identification
- **LOPA** — Layer of Protection Analysis
- **SIL** — Safety Integrity Level
- **PSV** — Pressure Safety Valve (relief valve)

## Mechanical and piping

- **NPS / DN** — Nominal Pipe Size / Diamètre Nominal (nominal size)
- **SCH** — Schedule (pipe wall thickness designation)
- **MAWP** — Maximum Allowable Working Pressure
- **NPSHa / NPSHr** — Net Positive Suction Head available / required
- **BEP** — Best Efficiency Point
- **PWHT** — Post-Weld Heat Treatment
- **CA** — Corrosion Allowance
- **RF / FF / RTJ** — Raised Face / Flat Face / Ring-Type Joint (flange facings)

## QA/QC

- **ITP** — Inspection and Test Plan
- **QCP** — Quality Control Plan
- **WPS** — Welding Procedure Specification
- **PQR** — Procedure Qualification Record
- **WPQ** — Welder Performance Qualification
- **NDT / NDE** — Non-Destructive Testing / Examination
- **RT / UT / MT / PT / VT** — Radiographic / Ultrasonic / Magnetic particle / Penetrant / Visual testing
- **PMI** — Positive Material Identification
- **NCR** — Non-Conformance Report
- **FAT / SAT** — Factory / Site Acceptance Test

## Procurement

- **MTO** — Material Take-Off
- **BOM** — Bill of Materials
- **MR** — Material Requisition
- **RFQ** — Request for Quotation
- **TBE / CBE** — Technical / Commercial Bid Evaluation
- **PO** — Purchase Order
- **VDRL** — Vendor Document Requirements List

## Electrical and instrumentation

- **VFD** — Variable Frequency Drive
- **MCC** — Motor Control Centre
- **IP** — Ingress Protection rating
- **DCS** — Distributed Control System
- **BPCS** — Basic Process Control System
- **SIS** — Safety Instrumented System
- **HART** — Highway Addressable Remote Transducer (digital protocol over 4–20 mA)
