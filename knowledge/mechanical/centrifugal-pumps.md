# Centrifugal Pump Fundamentals

## How a centrifugal pump works

A rotating impeller adds velocity to the liquid; the casing (volute or
diffuser) converts that velocity into pressure. The pump delivers
**head** (energy per unit weight of liquid, in metres) — the pressure rise
it produces depends on the liquid's density.

## Key datasheet terms

- **Rated flow** — the flow at which the pump is specified and guaranteed
  (the "rated point"). It usually includes a margin over normal flow.
- **Normal flow** — the expected everyday operating flow.
- **Minimum continuous flow** — the lowest flow the pump can run at
  continuously without overheating or damaging vibration; below it a
  minimum-flow bypass is needed.
- **Differential head** — head added by the pump at rated flow.
- **Suction / discharge pressure** — pressures at the pump nozzles.
- **Shut-off head** — head at zero flow; used to check that downstream
  piping and the casing can withstand the maximum pressure.
- **Casing design pressure / MAWP** — the pressure the casing is designed
  to withstand, typically covering maximum suction pressure plus shut-off
  differential pressure.

## BEP (Best Efficiency Point)

The flow at which the pump runs at its highest efficiency. Operating well
away from BEP increases vibration, recirculation and wear. A good selection
places the rated point reasonably close to BEP; the acceptable operating
region is defined by the pump standard, vendor, and project specification.

## NPSH

- **NPSHa (available)** — the net positive suction head the system
  provides at the pump suction, above the liquid's vapour pressure.
- **NPSHr (required)** — the head the pump needs to avoid unacceptable
  cavitation, determined by the vendor from test.
- NPSHa must exceed NPSHr with a margin. The required margin is set by the
  project specification or applicable pump standard.

Cavitation (vapour bubbles collapsing on the impeller) causes noise,
vibration, head loss and impeller erosion.

## Seals

Shaft sealing is usually by **mechanical seal**. The seal arrangement and
flush plan are chosen for the fluid's hazard, temperature and cleanliness.

## What to verify on a pump datasheet

1. Rated point (flow and head) matches the process data.
2. NPSHa vs NPSHr margin is adequate.
3. Casing design pressure covers maximum suction pressure + shut-off differential.
4. Materials suit the fluid (corrosion, erosion, temperature).
5. Driver rating covers the end-of-curve power, with the specified margin.
6. Test requirements (hydrotest, performance test, NPSH test) are stated.
