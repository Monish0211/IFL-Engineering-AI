## Length and size

- 1 inch = 25.4 mm; 1 ft = 0.3048 m.
- Pipe sizes are designated by a nominal size (NPS in inches, or DN in
  millimetres, e.g. NPS 4 ≈ DN 100). The nominal size is not the actual
  outside or inside diameter.

## Temperature

- °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
- K = °C + 273.15

## Power and energy

- 1 kW = 1.341 hp (mechanical horsepower)
- 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ

## Head and pressure (liquids)

Pressure and liquid head are related by the fluid density:

  ΔP = ρ · g · H

For water at about 20 °C (ρ ≈ 998 kg/m³), 10.2 m of head ≈ 1 bar. For
other liquids, divide by the specific gravity: head (m) ≈ 10.2 × ΔP (bar) / SG.

## Good practice

- Always state units, and whether pressure is gauge or absolute.
- Keep one unit system per calculation and convert at the end.
- Check project documents for the required unit system (SI, US customary, or mixed).
